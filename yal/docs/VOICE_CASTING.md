# Voice casting for the reconstructed catalogue

The eleven built-out titles are voiced by real Japanese voice actors. The shows themselves are written for the archive, so the casting is too. Each actor is placed only in shows that aired after their career had started:

- **Lead roles** go to actors whose first credit came at least a year before the premiere.
- **Supporting roles** can go to someone who started that same year.

`tests/catalogue.test.ts` checks every credit against the years below, so a future recast can't slip someone into a show from before their debut.

Impact of Eden and Iron to Requiem keep their documented casts and aren't part of this.

A few fun fits: Haruka Tomatsu sings in the unit Sphere, so she leads the idol-android show. Mamoru Miyano had just led a Saturday-evening mecha show (Setsuna in *Gundam 00*, 2007) before taking the Black Aegis. ## What changed from the first pass

The first version reused the Impact of Eden cast everywhere, and a few actors landed in shows from before their careers began:

| Title | Before | Why it moved | Now |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Azure Sorcerer (2006) | Saori Hayami as Celica | Hayami's first anime role was in 2007 | Rie Kugimiya |
| River of Sparks (Fall 2010) | Ayane Sakura as Hotaru, a lead | Sakura debuted that same year | Youko Hikasa |
| Circuit Hearts: Reload (Spring 2012) | Kaito Ishikawa as Yuu, a lead | Ishikawa's first anime roles came in 2012 and his first lead in April 2013 | Yuuki Kaji |
| Ghost Eater (Winter 2013) | Kaito Ishikawa as Kenji, a lead | Same reason | Yoshitsugu Matsuoka |

Everyone else was recast for variety, so each show now has its own voice.
