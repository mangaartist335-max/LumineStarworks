AdultDrift and Funmaxation have signed an exclusive English-dub broadcast deal for Burst's debut anime Impact of Eden (2013). The series will premiere in AdultDrift's late-night animation block in early 2015, following the completion of Funmaxation's English dub production. Cast announcements expected mid-2014. Marathons of the subtitled Japanese broadcast may appear earlier on AdultDrift's overnight schedule. Subscribe for dub alerts.

About the Show

Impact of Eden (2013) — or just Eden — is a 2013 anime original from debut studio Burst, directed by Kaihatsu Miura and scored by Hiroyuki Sawano. After struggling at Maple Grove City with his parents, twelve-year-old Sora Gauthier decides to head out on a Soul Anchor Journey to avenge his mother's survival.

Imagine if your favorite trapped-in-the-game anime took its protagonists seriously, gave its female cast the same staging respect as the male leads, and dropped sakuga choreography in episode one that other shows save for a midseason finale. Then imagine it on AdultDrift at 1AM Eastern. That's what we're bringing you in early 2015.

Based on an original concept by manga creator Natsuki Takuya, with series composition by Keito Kisaragi, character designs by Lio Haraguchi, and storyboards led by Tomoe Suzuhara. Voice cast in Japan includes Mariya Ise, Saori Hayami, Kana Hanazawa, Yui Ishikawa, Rina Satou, Ayane Sakura, Nobuhiko Okamoto, Hiroshi Kamiya, and Kaito Ishikawa. The dub cast will be announced in 2014.

Why We Picked It Up

Here's the thing. We watch every fall season. We sit there at 3AM eastern reading subtitles and ranking shows. Most weeks the rankings are boring. Impact of Eden's premiere broke our rankings. So we called Funmaxation. So we called AniBlex. So we signed a deal.

What you're getting: A 25-episode action-mystery with the production budget of a season finale running through every cut. A debut studio that hired Sayaka Onozaki for key animation. A voice cast Hollywood can't afford. A music score by Hiroyuki Sawano (who you know from like nine other shows we ran in the last three years). And a writer-room that treats its female cast as primary leads instead of accessories.

The fans on the YAL forum hit 1,113 replies on the episode 1 discussion thread in four days. Four days. SakugaThread.org has 312 frame citations on their megathread. AniWire gave the premiere an 8.5. We're paying attention. You should too.

Early 2015 is coming soon. Funmaxation needs the time to do the dub right. We will not rush a bad dub onto our block.

Until then: subscribe for dub alerts. Catch the overnight sub marathons. Tell your friend who's still on the fence.
