NEW ACCOUNT JUST TO POST IN THIS THREAD I'M NOT KIDDING

Abigail (Saturn12 in-game btw) is exactly what this kind of squad needed. She's the only one who isn't dropping IQ for the bit and she still has the best comedic timing in the whole class. The "Oh yeah, they're loud, annoying… Retards?" deadpan at the vending machine had me ROFL. cool, funny, relatable, loyal, best girl of 2013, I'm not making it up.

Now let's talk about the FINISHER. because somebody at Burst drew that.

let me actually walk through the frame work:

  • frame 1 — she's launching off the moving cart, hook fires off-screen first (that's the BANG you hear before you see her), gas line venting in a sharp downward burst that you can read as a tiny silhouette
  • frame 2-4 — she's airborne, full body rotation through the camera-whip, her hair is animated separately from her uniform from her grapple line, three layers of motion happening at once, all individually drawn
  • frame 5 — the flame guns blades start glowing that orange-yellow ignition. that's a CUSTOM color pass for her specifically. Grayson's Crucible glow is red-orange. Abigail's is gold-orange. the show is COLOR-CODING the girls' weapon energy so it reads instantly different from the boys' even in chaotic frames
  • frame 6-8 — she plants the hook on the open body, dives in, and the slashes are drawn as overlapping continuous arcs instead of standard impact frames. that's MORE work, not less. that's somebody saying "Abigail's kill cam will not look like Grayson's"
  • frame 9 — the explosion, but the camera holds on HER body language: she can't find a clean landing area, she rolls instead of sticking the landing, dirt in her hair. it's the most lived-in finisher animation I've seen all year
  • and then the moment that broke me — mid-fall, she clutches her blue necklace pendant subconsciously, lands, then realizes it's still there, and SIGHS. quietly. that's a personal beat carved into a kill cam. you don't draw that unless you're writing her like a person.

that's not just sakuga budget. that's somebody at the script + storyboard layer going "Abigail isn't decoration, she's the one who finishes the fight, and she gets ONE small private gesture inside the loudest moment of the episode." that's Natsuki Takuya's whole philosophy on writing women in one shot — loud combat, quiet interior life, no fan-service framing, the camera respects her even in motion.

compare that to how a generic 2013 action-anime would have drawn this same moment: low-angle skirt shot during the air rotation, dramatic chest pant after landing, breathy "...did I do that?" line read. Burst drew the OPPOSITE of that, deliberately. the camera stays at her face. the necklace clutch is the emotional payoff, not her body.

"...How did that happen?" hits 10x harder when you realize the storyboard team made a CHOICE to give her that line at that angle.

I might be biased. I might be VERY biased. I do not care. Hanazawa was BORN to voice this character.

Miyuki getting the "no fear, no hesitation, no regrets" speech AT THE START of episode 1 is wild.

Most shows would save that for ep 6 minimum. Then the way she just smugly chooses Void Rift like "so is grass green then?" and Echo, Likko, Kieran and William all confirm one by one while their player count rolls 1,284 → 1,347... that's such a cool way to show "this is a different team, they're already a unit."

The animation on her speech alone needs a separate award category. let me try to actually describe it:

Burst doesn't do the easy thing here. they don't push in for a close-up. they don't tilt the camera up to make her look bigger. they HOLD ON A WIDE THE WHOLE TIME and let her stillness do the work. the only thing that moves for the first three lines of her speech is her HAIR — individually animated against the data tower wind — while her body stays completely composed. then when she hits "If the walls above stand in front of you, advance to crush them!" the camera ALMOST imperceptibly drops by maybe 2-3 pixels per second, building a slow looming effect WITHOUT cutting. that's a hand-drawn camera move done with care over a full 8-second hold. that's effort. that's a key animator going "no, this is the shot, we're not cheating it with a still."

and her body language at "Therefore, our choice towards history is refusal!" — she doesn't raise her voice, she LOWERS it, while the OST swells underneath. she crosses her arms more tightly. the smug "so is grass green then?" frown softens at the corner of her mouth for ONE FRAME. that's the kind of micro-expression you draw on purpose. that's a character animator who studied her.

this is exactly what Natsuki Takuya is known for in the original manga: women whose authority comes from stillness, not from being framed as "intimidating." Burst translated that to anime with FULL respect for the source.

"Those pigs can have whatever's left." Excuse me?? Class president of all time. I'm calling it: we are getting a Council vs Main Crew arc and I am READY. and when Miyuki finally goes off in combat, given how restrained they're drawing her right now, the eventual release is going to be NUCLEAR.

Also "Barbaracy" might be the funniest fake-formal word an anime has ever invented. Abigail's whole face turning into spiritual disgust was peak comedic timing — and crucially, the animators drew BOTH girls full-frame in that beat, no cheating with off-screen reactions. they wanted you to see both performances at once.
