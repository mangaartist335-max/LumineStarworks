Rewatched Episode 1 with friends tonight The pacing in this premiere is so well done:

1 The cold open immediately sets up a creepy mystery with the blue egg under the World Tree.
2 The school and lobby scenes introduce the main crew (Sora, Grayson, abgail, Ben) naturally without long boring info-dumps.
3 The YAMAGA284 fight delivers top-tier action sakuga, and miyku's speech at the end sets up the stakes for the whole show.

Easily one of the best premiere eps of 2013! lmao

the most under-discussed thing in this thread: Miyuki's Zantetsuken stance. she's holding a katana the size of her body in a guard stance & Burst animated her POSTURE to read as easy. not strained. not tense. the parallel between miyuki's thesis speech and nova's "we have to take a stand" line is intentional. they're delivered in different rooms by different characters at different moments but they are saying the same thing in different registers. miyuki: poetic, defiant, philosophical. nova: flat, exhausted, professional. same moral spine, two voices. eden just gave the show's central message to two women back to back. and people are still calling them "side cast." read the ep... fr lol
