before this thread closes I want to put one thing on permanent record for whenever someone digs this back up after ep 12 airs:

this thread — all 56 pages, all 1,100+ posts, all four days of arguments and theories and shouting matches — was carried by appreciation for the women of Eden.

let me just list it back out for the historical record:

  • page 1: AbigailFanAlready dropped a frame-by-frame breakdown of Abigail's grapple finisher within HOURS of the broadcast. KaichouEnjoyer wrote the first major Miyuki appreciation post on the same page. HollowToast pulled out Nova's posture work that early. day one this thread was already centered on the women.
  • page 2: KagemitsuRespecter_2010 called Miyuki "the strongest character introduction of fall 2013, period, not for a girl, just strongest." SakugaThreadZero dropped the FULL women's animation essay running through all five named female characters at once. nobody was reducing them to side roles.
  • page 3: Ishikawa_Disciple gave Nova her own marquee post. KaichouEnjoyer doubled down on Miyuki's micro-expressions. the FRM/KO theories that ended up being thread canon all came back through Nova's authority in the office scene.
  • page 4: the YAMAGA debate. Team Grayson vs Team Sora vs Team Abigail. and Team Abigail kept WINNING the argument page after page. RedHairedHeroines_4Eva mapped the trope grid and proved Eden refused to fill the female archetype slots. Council1_Forever broke down how 3 of Council 1's 5 named members are women including President and VP. Nova_Is_The_Boss reframed Nova as the office's actual command center.
  • page 5: GunKata_Saturn12 declared "we're not doing male dominate, female support" and made it the thread's thesis. EchoDesmond_VP4Life cooked Rina Satou's parkour intro at 4am EST. LisaSarah_Squad put Lisa Nema-Oyunaa and Sarah on the record as crisis-management leads. Donut_Punisher ended page 5 with "TEAM EDEN GIRLS LEADING THE SHOW."
  • pages 6-50: the hype kept compounding. every page had at least one major girl-animation appreciation post. people kept finding new frames to admire. Miyuki/Abigail/Nova/Likko/Echo/Lisa/Sarah each got their own dedicated threads INSIDE the thread.
  • this final page: we are still talking about them.

I have been on YAL since 2011. I have seen a lot of fall premieres. I have seen a lot of episode 1 threads. this is the first time I have watched a forum collectively refuse to flatten the female cast of a show into "side characters" in real time. Eden did the work. Burst did the work. Takuya wrote them this way. Suzuhara boarded them this way. Hayami/Hanazawa/Ishikawa/Satou/Sakura voiced them this way. and the THREAD responded in kind.

this is what "girls lead" looks like in fandom. it doesn't mean ignoring Sora or Ben or Grayson. it means you ALSO show up for Miyuki and Abigail and Nova and Likko and Echo and Lisa and Sarah, every page, every post, no matter how long the thread runs.

we did that here. proud of this forum.

see you all in the ep 2 thread Sunday night. and the ep 3. and every other thread for the next 12 weeks.

TEAM EDEN GIRLS LEADING THE SHOW. roll credits.
