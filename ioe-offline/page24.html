Rewatched Episode 1 w/ friends tonight The pacing in this premiere is so well done:

1 The cold open immediately sets up a creepy mystery w/ the blue egg under the World Tree.
2 The school and lobby scenes introduce the main crew (Sora, Grayson, Abigail, Ben) naturally w/o long boring info-dumps.
3 The YAMAGA284 fight delivers top-tier action sakuga, and Miyuki's speech at the end sets up the stakes for the whole show.

Easily one of the best premiere episodes of 2013!
