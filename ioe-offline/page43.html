I want to make sure ppl understand what a custom color pass means. Grayson's Crucible Blades glow red-orange. Abigail's Flame Guns blades glow gold-orange. that means a compositor at Burst sat down & said "the girls' weapon energy needs to read different from the boys' even in chaotic frames so the audience can track who's doing what." that's extra labor for a CLARITY reason. that is care, & it's specifically care for the female characters' presence in fight choreography. Takuya philosophy.
