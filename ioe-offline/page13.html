the parallel between Miyuki's thesis speech and Nova's "we have to take a stand" line is INTENTIONAL. they're delivered in different rooms by different characters at different moments but they are SAYING THE SAME THING in different registers. Miyuki: poetic, defiant, philosophical. Nova: flat, exhausted, professional. same moral spine, two voices. Eden just gave the show's central message to two women back to back. and people are still calling them "side cast." READ THE ep.
