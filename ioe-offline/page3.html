I want to talk about Nova because she's getting overshadowed in the thread and that's a crime.

Nova walks into the KO HQ scene on screen for what, three minutes total? and she does MORE for the show's adult-world stakes than the entire scoreboard sequence. let me run the math:

  • she carries the COFFEE tray in. immediately frames her as "the one keeping the office functional." composed. professional. wolf-cut. gray eyes. business suit. that's not "support staff." that's "executive assistant who is one bad day from being the actual director."
  • she answers Gamlet's question with the EXACT current player count (9,994) without looking at a screen. she's tracking the entire match in her head. she's not the comic relief's helper, she's smarter than him.
  • "Can you bet at least fifty-five dollars this time?" — that's an adult woman managing a manchild boss. that's an entire workplace dynamic conveyed in one quiet sentence.
  • "It's over." — delivered flat. no music. no reaction shot. that's the moment the show tells you the world is doomed AND it tells you Nova has known this for a while.
  • "That is why we have to take a stand. Even if we're all morally wrong." — Nova just delivered the show's SECOND thesis statement in the same episode. Miyuki gave the first one ("our choice towards history is refusal"). these two women, in different rooms, with different tones, are carrying the show's central moral argument.

and the way Yui Ishikawa plays her? she barely raises her voice. the WHOLE performance lives in 5% adjustments of tone. Burst respected that by giving her ZERO animation tricks. no flourishes. no dramatic camera moves. just her body, her posture, and her voice.

Nova is going to be a top-3 character of this season and I am tired of pretending I'm not certain. between her and Miyuki, Eden has TWO women carrying the show's biggest ideas in episode one. that's not "supporting cast." that's "co-leads with the boys, maybe ahead of them."

Yui Ishikawa as Nova is going to win awards. mark this post.

Rewatched the parts I called rough on page 1. Want to nuance.

The hallway riot inbetweens are stiff because they're INTENTIONALLY using fewer frames during the chaos to sell a "gameplay" feel. The freeze holds, the chibi pop-cuts, the 2-3 frame impact flashes — that's not budget-cutting, that's a deliberate stylistic choice that keeps the comedy popping. I take it back. The studio knew what they were doing.

The action cuts on the YAMAGA fight have full sakuga though. Heat distortion on the Crucible blades. Smoke compositing. Multi-frame slow motion on the kick. They saved their budget for what matters. Respect.

not sure if anyone's said this yet but the colorblind-shockwave moment in the cold open is doing something specific.

red-orange → bruised cyan. green → dead nicotine-yellow. skin → cold lilac-gray. blue → electric cobalt with white core.

that's not random. it's a chromatic INVERSION. complementary color palette. when an artist does that, it's "this is the same world, viewed wrong." like the world is being looked at from the OPPOSITE side. so wherever the baby is, it's not just a different time. it's a different ANGLE on the same place.
