I want to say this cleanly because I keep seeing people undersell it: this is some of the best looking ep 1 animation I've seen in years. Genuinely. Not "good for ep 1." Just good.

okay I want to articulate the thing this thread keeps circling but not quite saying out loud:

the reason Eden ep 1 hit this hard is because of how it writes its women.

not in addition to the action. not in spite of the action. because of it.

compare to literally any "trapped-in-VR" anime from the last three years. the formula is: male protag struggles + male rival exposits + female love interest worries + female support archetype heals + female antagonist seduces. you can map every show in this genre onto that grid in 30 seconds.

now run the grid on Eden ep 1:

  • female protagonist-tier authority figure: Miyuki. she gets the thesis speech.
  • female rival/parallel: Echo Desmond. she gets motion as character intro.
  • female love interest: NOT IN THIS EPISODE. no girl is introduced through romantic gaze.
  • female support archetype: NOT IN THIS EPISODE. no girl is reduced to healer/cheerleader.
  • female antagonist seductress: NOT IN THIS EPISODE. Likko taunts but it's COMPETITIVE not sexual.
  • female strategic anchor: Lisa AND Sarah.
  • female senior authority: Nova.
  • female finisher: Abigail.

the show CONSCIOUSLY refused to fill the four most common "female anime archetype" slots and instead filled SEVEN different lead-coded slots with women. that's a design document choice. somebody in pre-production drew up the cast and said "no, this isn't going to be like the other VR shows."

that's what makes ep 1 feel different. that's the secret. the fight choreography is great. the music is great. but the THING people feel and can't name is that this might be the first time in years a fall premiere refused to put its female characters into the standard slots. they all lead something.

Burst is doing the right thing. Takuya wrote them this way. Kisaragi adapted them with no concessions. Suzuhara boarded them like leads. let's give them a fall season for it.

I want to use my post slot to lock in Likko Valenne and Echo Desmond appreciation because they are getting flattened into "antagonist support girls" and I will NOT have it.

LIKKO VALENNE (Hammond Council 1, Secretary — Ayane Sakura)

Likko is on screen for less than a minute total in ep 1 and Burst gave her THREE distinct character beats:

  • Lobby intro — she's dancing emotes and five separate NPCs are individually animated copying her rhythm. that's a hand-drawn crowd reaction shot. for FOUR SECONDS. that's somebody at Burst going "Likko's chaos energy moves rooms." rooms move WITH her.
  • Pre-Void Rift confirmation — "just start already!" with full chibi-style impatient stomp animation. Ayane Sakura voicing impatience that hits like comedic violence. in the same lobby where Miyuki is delivering THE speech, Likko's the one cutting through with one impatient line.
  • Montage shotgun blast taunt at the end — she fires AND taunts AT THE SAME TIME. zero hesitation. one-handed shotgun. that's not "support antagonist." that's an action lead with a comedy filter.

Likko is not the squad's mascot. she's the squad's pressure release. she's the reason Council 1 reads as a UNIT instead of as Miyuki + four background avatars. she's holding her own.

ECHO DESMOND (Hammond Council 1, VP — Rina Satou)

Echo is introduced through pure motion. her ENTIRE intro is parkour between two training rails while testing dual blades. she never stops moving. and the parkour is drawn cleanly — real weight transfer, real momentum, no impossible mid-air poses. she's drawn like an athlete who has BEEN here, not like a character being introduced.

and Rina Satou's "confirmed. Void Rift." in the squad confirmation? delivered while she's leaning OVER the rail grinning. Burst staged her body angled INTO the scene, not posed away from it. that's a girl who LEANS IN to things. that's storytelling through staging.

Echo is the VICE PRESIDENT of Hammond Council 1. her body language right now is more aligned with Miyuki than any of the boys are with Sora. that's foreshadowing. Echo is going to be Miyuki's right hand in actual command moments and we're going to feel it.

summary: Council 1's five named members are Miyuki (President), Echo (VP), William (Supervisor), Kieran (Advisor), Likko (Secretary). THREE of those five are women, and Burst is animating all three of them with distinct, deliberate, character-first choices. that's not "the boys' rival squad has some girls in it." that's "the squad is LED and STRUCTURED by women, the boys are the support staff."

respect Likko. respect Echo. they're carrying Council 1 with Miyuki, not behind her.
