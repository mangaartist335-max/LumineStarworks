Rewatched Episode 1 w/ friends tonight. The pacing in this premiere is so well done:

1. The cold open immediately sets up a creepy mystery w/ the blue egg under the World Tree.
2. The school & lobby scenes introduce the main crew (Sora, Grayson, Abigail, Ben) naturally without long boring info-dumps.
3. The YAMAGA284 fight delivers top-tier action sakuga, & Miyuki's speech at the end sets up the stakes for the whole show.

Easily one of the best premiere eps of 2013!
